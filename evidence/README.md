## 1. Borrowers on real 2026 prices (REPLAY)

One position a day (2026-01-01 to 2026-11-05) with $10,000 of stablecoin debt, sitting exactly at Stead's trigger (HF 1.15) at 00:00 UTC, followed hour by hour on real prices (Binance hourly closes), for the 24 h buy-back window and for a week. After a claim ends, the rescued position is followed on: bought back, it keeps the top-up; settled, it sits at HF 1.5; a later liquidation is charged on the full debt.

- **Without Stead:** Aave liquidates the first hour its health is below 1; the borrower loses bonus × 50 % × debt.
- **With Stead:** the stewards' real strategies bid; the lowest premiums top the position up to HF 1.5. The claim then ends in a buy-back at 24 h, an early settlement at the oracle price (own HF net of the claim below 1.1), or an Aave liquidation (premiums forfeited). The cost is the premium, in units of the collateral, at the exit price.
- **"Stead cost more"** counts the days the price recovered: the borrower paid a premium for a rescue that, in hindsight, wasn't needed. It is the price of the insurance and is shown, not hidden.
- **Rule violations** checks every path: the premium at the trigger price is at most Aave's liquidation cost, and the claim is owed exactly the supplied units plus the premium units.
- Liquidation threshold and bonus: read from the Aave V3 Ethereum Pool (`data/aave-reserves.json`). Not modelled: interest over the day, the price impact of an Aave liquidation, a borrower who adds collateral on their own.
